## 4. Workaround 1 — Predict Seats, Not Counts (`x7_2s_train_presence_seats.py`)

Seat occupancy varies *within* the 1p/2p/3p days (on the 2p day seat 1 is full
in seats12/13/14 and empty in seats23/24/34). With per-day 50/50 re-weighting
the day carries no information about any seat label; count = sum of seats.
Train on 1p-3p days, test on 0p/4p days = unseen days *and* unseen counts.

```
                          seat acc   count acc   predicted
1p+2p days -> 3p day        40.2%       4.5%     mostly 1-2
1p+3p days -> 2p day        44.9%      60.3%     mostly 2
2p+3p days -> 1p day        49.2%       3.0%     mostly 2-3
1p-3p days -> 0p day        49.0%       0.0%     mostly 2
1p-3p days -> 4p day        52.0%       0.0%     mostly 2
control (bins > 2.5 m)     41-54%     same collapse
```

Chance on every unseen day. Within a day the same model looked perfect
(94-100%) — see §7 for why that was also false.

## 7. The Mechanism — Block Recognition

Hold out one whole arrangement within a day, train on the others:

```
2p day   mean seat acc 49.7%   (chance 50%)
3p day   mean seat acc 44.8%
the model always predicts one of the arrangements it has already seen
```

The within-day 94-100% was recognition of the 50-trial block. Every label in v2
is tied to a contiguous stretch of time — count to the day, arrangement to its
block.

**Physics:** at λ ≈ 41 mm, 1 cm of torso displacement rotates round-trip phase
~90°. Each sit-down lands a few mm differently and yields a unique phase
pattern that is stable for all 50 trials. Eight of the ten CNN channels are
absolute phase (the AoA phasors added Sep 15). **v2 therefore has ~16
independent samples**, not 799.

## 8. Why Energy and Breathing Rate Cannot Count

```
same sit-down, trial to trial:   median CV 108%  (43-317%)
one person alone (1p day):       seat1 -1.1 dB  seat2 0.0 dB  seat3 -13.1 dB  seat4 -7.2 dB
adding a back person to a front person:   +13% power
```

Adding a person changes energy by 13%; repeating the same trial changes it by
108%. Same-row occupants share range, so peaks count rows. Breathing *rate*
cannot separate people: in 10 s the whole adult band fits in ~1 FFT bin, and
even at 60 s two people are often within 1-2 breaths/min.

**Training-free breathing detector** (breathing-band / 0.8-2.5 Hz power, per
bin, max per row):

```
FRONT  empty  -0.38 (0p) -0.21 (1p) -0.11 (2p)   occupied -0.12 (4p) 0.00 (3p) 0.25 (1p) 0.42 (2p)
BACK   empty  -0.03 (0p)  0.16 (1p)  0.25 (2p)   occupied -0.14 (4p) -0.09 (3p) 0.07 (1p) 0.20 (2p)
```

Front row: correct direction on every day — the first cross-day-consistent
signal in the dataset. Back row: fails even within a day (front-row breathing
leaks into back bins; fidgeting inflates the denominator).

## 10. Independent-Source Count (Eigen-Analysis)

Breathing-band spatial covariance over 2 sensors x 4 virtual channels x seat
bins, each channel whitened by its own noise band, pooled per sit-down.

First attempt (Hann taper): **77 components in the empty room** — slow drift
leaked into the 0.2 Hz bin, which is only 2 bins from DC in a 10 s window.
With a quadratic detrend instead:

```
                    top whitened eigenvalue   components
empty room                   7.4                  1
any occupied             15.9 - 27.7            4 - 7
by count   1p: 4,5,4,4   2p: 6,5,7,7,6,5   3p: 5,5,5,6   4p: 4
```

Presence separates cleanly and training-free. Counting does not: one person
yields 4-5 components (nonlinear phase modulation, several scatterers) and the
count falls at 3-4 people, since sensors 15-20 cm apart see nearly the same
mixture.

## 11. Delivered — `x7_presence_zone.py`

**Scope agreed with the user:** occupied vs empty + front/back row;
lightweight for car hardware; **a missed occupant is worse than a false alarm**.

```
frames (5 Hz) -> 10 s blocks: quadratic detrend, FFT
             -> zones: front 0.35-1.05 m (80 ch), back 1.25-2.0 m (88 ch)
             -> per zone: log(lambda1 / Marchenko-Pastur edge), #components,
                max breathing/reference, max breathing/own-noise, motion
             -> + front/back coherence (leak rejection)        11 features
             -> 3 logistic regressions                        36 parameters
             -> state machine
```

Reference floor = bins behind the antenna (no occupant possible, in a room or a
car). Block FFTs rather than streaming filters, so offline training and live use
run *identical* code.

**Safety policy:** thresholds at ≥99% out-of-fold recall; "occupied" enters at
once (probability or per-frame motion) and is held **60 s** after the last
evidence; warm-up reports `UNKNOWN`; stalled frames report `SENSOR FAULT`;
neither ever reports `EMPTY`. Outputs that cannot meet the recall target with
<50% false alarm are marked unreliable, shown as `?`, and never drive
"occupied".

### Results (30 s window, auto-selected)

| output | status | validation | AUC | false alarm at 99% recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **front row** | usable | leave-one-**day**-out | **0.998** | **4.8%** |
| occupied | usable, **not trustworthy** | leave-one-sit-down-out | 1.000 | 0.0% |
| back row | **unreliable** | leave-one-day-out | 0.920 | 83.6% |

**Control:** the same pipeline on 2.6-3.9 m (no seats) predicts occupied at
AUC **0.985** — the empty session differs by more than the absence of people
(§6). Recorded as a caveat in the model file.

### Behaviour (replay, 16 runs x ~200 s)

```
missed-occupant 0.1%    false-occupied 11.1%  (the 60 s hold, by design)
OCCUPIED after arrival:   median 1 s  (flattered by recording seams)
EMPTY after departure:    88 s  (30 s window + 60 s hold)
compute: 0.01 ms/frame, 8 ms/decision at 1 Hz, desktop Python
```

## 12. Errors and Corrections During This Session

1. **"Who sat where" hypothesis** — proposed as the cause of cross-day failure;
   refuted by the user (same person, same seat, every day).
2. **Premature PoC claim** — within-session 94-100% seat detection was
   presented as a defensible result, then shown to be block recognition (§7)
   and withdrawn.
3. **Sit-down grouping** — a 30 s gap rule split sit-downs into 31 groups,
   leaking one fingerprint across folds. Regrouped by scenario + sitting (17).
4. **Window selection** — the first auto-selection picked 60 s because of the
   unusable back row, although the front row was worse there (11.9% vs 4.8%).
   Unusable outputs are now excluded from selection.
5. **Back row driving "occupied"** — would have made an empty cabin read
   occupied most of the time. Gated by the usability flag.
6. **Eigen-analysis taper** — 77 "sources" in an empty room; fixed with
   detrending (§10).
7. **Replay design** — shuffled single files never exercised transitions;
   rewritten to play sit-down runs. Its arrival latency is still inflated by the
   seams between recordings.
8. **Tooling** — bash heredocs mangled `\n` in two patch scripts (both aborted
   on assertion before writing); patches were moved to files.

## 15. Lessons

**Count independent samples, not files.** 799 recordings were ~16 sit-downs. Every
model with enough capacity found that out before we did.

**Prefer features the fingerprint cannot reach.** Absolute phase identifies a
sit-down; ratios, whitened eigenvalues and detection statistics do not. The only
cross-day result came from the model with the fewest parameters.

**A control is part of every result.** The non-seat control exposed the
0p-session difference that the headline 1.000 AUC hid.

**Encode the safety asymmetry in the state machine, not the classifier.** Recall
targets, the exit hold, `UNKNOWN` and `SENSOR FAULT` are what make "a missed
occupant is worse" true in code.
