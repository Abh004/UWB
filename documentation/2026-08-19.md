# Engineering Session Summary: Dual-Sensor Human Presence & Occupancy Detection
**Date:** August 19, 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Today, the project expanded from gesture recognition into **Dual-Sensor Human Presence & Occupancy Counting** (0, 1, 2, and 3 people). We built a complete end-to-end pipeline including dual-radar presence data recording, a custom 2D Convolutional Neural Network architecture in PyTorch, intra-class physics-based synthetic dataset generation, and an offline evaluation framework with confusion matrix analysis.

---

## 2. What We Built Today

### A. Dual-Sensor Presence Data Recorder (`x7_2s_record_presence.py`)
- **Objective:** Capture synchronized dual-sensor IQ radar baseband data for human occupancy.
- **Key Parameters:**
  - **339 Range Bins:** Extends detection range to ~2.2 meters depth (at 6.5 mm per bin).
  - **10 FPS:** Tuned for human presence and breathing detection.
  - **5.0 Seconds per Trial:** (50 frames per `.npy` file).
  - **Preset:** Automated configuration using `human_preset.json`.
  - **Multi-Trial Batching:** Added user prompt to record $N$ trials sequentially.
- **Dataset Collected (`human_dataset_2s/`):**
  - `0_people` (Empty Room): 100 samples
  - `1_people` (1 Person): 100 samples
  - `2_people` (2 People): 151 samples
  - `3_people` (3 People): 100 samples
  - **Total Real Recordings:** 451 samples

### B. Dual-Sensor Presence CNN Architecture (`x7_2s_train_presence.py`)
- **Model:** `PresenceCNN_X7_Dual`
  - **Input Tensor:** `(Batch, Channels=2, Height=50, Width=339)` representing `(Sensors, Time, Range)`.
  - **Feature Extraction:** 4 Conv2D blocks (16 $\rightarrow$ 32 $\rightarrow$ 64 $\rightarrow$ 128 channels) with BatchNorm, ReLU, and MaxPool2D.
  - **Classification Head:** Adaptive Average Pooling $\rightarrow$ Dropout(0.5) $\rightarrow$ Linear classifier outputting class logits.
- **Clutter Rejection:** Moving Target Indicator (MTI) temporal mean subtraction to filter out static walls and furniture.

### C. Intra-Class Synthetic Radar Dataset Generator (`x7_2s_synthesize_data.py`)
- **Objective:** Expand dataset volume without requiring manual recording hours, adhering strictly to class-preserving synthesis.
- **Holdout Test Separation:** Automatically isolated 20% of pure real recordings (90 samples) into `human_dataset_2s_test_holdout/` to ensure zero data leakage.
- **Transformations Applied:**
  1. **Range Translation:** Bounded shift ($\pm 15$ range bins) simulating distance variation.
  2. **Temporal Cyclic Rolling:** Phase-shifting breathing cycles continuously across 50 frames.
  3. **Complex Phase Rotation & Micro-Doppler Modulation:** Simulates breathing rate variation (0.18–0.45 Hz) and sub-wavelength radar reflections.
  4. **Amplitude Scaling:** Models variations in body size, clothing reflectivity, and angle of arrival.
  5. **Complex Thermal Noise Injection.**
- **Output:** Generated a balanced training dataset of **2,400 samples (600 per class)** in `human_dataset_2s_augmented/`.

### D. Offline Evaluation & Confusion Matrix Tool (`x7_2s_eval_presence.py`)
- Automated validation tool computing Overall Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and a complete Confusion Matrix against unseen real-world test data.

## 3. Issues Encountered & Solutions

### Issue 1: Per-Sample Normalization Flattening Energy Levels
- **Symptom:** During initial holdout evaluation, the model achieved **51.11% accuracy** on real test samples. While it achieved 100% recall on empty rooms (`0_people`) and 80% on `3_people`, it struggled to differentiate `1_people` vs `2_people`.
- **Root Cause Analysis:** In the initial preprocessing, `(mag - mean) / std` was applied per sample. This normalized every sample to zero-mean and unit variance, destroying the total reflected energy difference (where 2 people reflect roughly twice the energy of 1 person).
- **Fix:** Replaced per-sample standard deviation normalization with `np.log1p(mag * 100.0)`. This compresses dynamic range for stable gradient descent while strictly preserving absolute reflection energy differences between 1, 2, and 3 people.
